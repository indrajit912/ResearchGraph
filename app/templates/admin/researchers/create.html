Data Entry Guidelines

As a moderator, you are responsible for maintaining the accuracy and privacy of the ResearchGraph database.

  • Public Data Only: Do NOT add any private information. Only input data that is already publicly available on the internet.
  • Best Practices: The safest source of truth is arXiv.org. Check their latest pre-prints to verify their current affiliations and institutional email addresses.
  • Affiliations Change: Researchers frequently move between departments or universities. Double check before assigning an affiliation.
  • PhD Details: For accurate PhD Institutes and graduation years, you may consult the Mathematics Genealogy Project.
