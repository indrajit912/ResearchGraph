How to get started:

There is a very high chance that your profile is already curated in our database. To claim your identity:

  1. Search for your name or academic email using the bar below.
  2. Check the results and click "Claim This Profile" on your card.
  3. We will send a verification link to the email address on file.
  4. Once verified, you can immediately begin adding your collaborators!
